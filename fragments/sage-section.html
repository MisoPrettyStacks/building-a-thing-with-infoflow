Stablecoin flow — slow liquidity tide experimental

Sage reads the 24-hour market-cap change of USDT and USDC from the free CoinGecko API, adds them together, and turns the combined change into a gentle liquidity tilt. Rising supply means fresh fiat parked on-chain where it can buy (liquidity entering — mild bullish); falling supply means fiat leaving (mild bearish). The read refreshes at most once an hour and rests on the cache between fetches. The member emits a slow liquidity tilt — currently at weight zero, so it is scored but never affects the forecast until Sage finds out-of-sample evidence.
