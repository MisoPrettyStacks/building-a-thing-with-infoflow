Her APPLY verdicts send the signal to formal out-of-sample testing: nothing gets model weight without passing. Her rule is conditional by design: she follows BTC only when the pair is coupled.

Correlation regime — BTC coupling experimental

Cherry aligns the lab's own XRP and BTC 5-minute candles by timestamp and measures the rolling correlation of their returns over the last day. When the pair is coupled, BTC's recent drift becomes a conditional tilt on XRP — currently at weight zero, so it is scored but never affects the forecast until Cherry finds out-of-sample evidence. When they decouple, she abstains: following BTC then would be importing someone else's noise.

How it works: every cycle the runner pairs XRP and BTC bars by exact timestamp, computes log returns, and takes the correlation over the last day of shared bars. Only in a coupled regime does BTC's recent drift become a tilt — and only if that drift is expressive, not a whisper. Honest caveat: correlations regime-shift — altcoins decouple on idiosyncratic news and re-couple on macro moves, so conditionality is the signal, not the correlation itself. The live scoreboard above is the verdict, not the theory.
