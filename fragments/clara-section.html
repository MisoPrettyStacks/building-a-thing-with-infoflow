How it works: Every cycle Clara separates boundary bars (:00/:15/:30/:45 starts) from off-grid bars. A read is licensed only when the last boundary bar arrived with a volume burst and recent boundary bars agree in sign — the configuration periodic algorithmic flow would leave behind. Scored out-of-sample; a quiet grid earns an abstention, not a story.
