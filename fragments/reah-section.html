Meet Reah — an agentic agent, not a mascot

Reah watches for moves that have gone too far. When XRP lurches on aggressive volume, she asks whether the move overshot — and leans the other way, a little. When nothing expressive just happened, she says so and abstains.

Titles: Principal Investigator of the Mean Reversion Lab · Contrarian Analyst · Research Scientist.

Duties: Watch the last closed bars, measure move size against volume, guard against fading whispers, and keep the lab notebook.

How it works: Every cycle Reah reads the lab's own closed XRP bars. A fade is licensed only when the recent move is expressive — ideally on aggressive volume, the kind of taker-driven move the literature finds tends to snap back. The tilt is small, capped, and scored out-of-sample like every other member: theory is cheap, the scoreboard decides.
