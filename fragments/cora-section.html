Cross-asset momentum — ETH/SOL spillover experimental

Cora watches the two biggest altcoins as bellwethers of broad crypto risk appetite. For each she compares its recent drift against its own recent volatility — so a wild coin and a calm coin speak the same language — and blends the two into a small bounded tilt: positive when broad momentum is up (a tailwind for XRP), negative when it's down (a headwind). Currently at weight zero, so it is scored but never affects the forecast until Cora finds out-of-sample evidence.

How it works: every cycle the runner reads recent 5-minute ETH-USD and SOL-USD candles from the public Coinbase exchange API (no keys), measures each asset's trailing drift relative to its own trailing volatility, and blends them into one tilt. The tilt is deliberately slow and bounded — it hangs in there across many windows until the cross-asset regime genuinely changes. Honest caveat: momentum is the most crowded anomaly in finance — by the time a move is obvious on the majors, the spillover into XRP has often already happened. The live scoreboard above is the verdict, not the theory.
