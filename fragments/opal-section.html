Order-book depth — live microstructure signal experimental

Opal reads the live Coinbase XRP-USD order book every cycle. When resting buy interest outweighs resting sell interest, that's demand pressure; when sell interest outweighs buy interest, that's supply pressure. The spread tells her how healthy the book is, and usable depth tells her how much pressure the book can absorb. The member emits a small tilt when the book genuinely takes a side — currently at weight zero, so it is scored but never affects the forecast until Opal finds out-of-sample evidence.

How it works: every cycle the runner pulls one live snapshot of the XRP-USD level-2 book from Coinbase's public API (no keys) and measures mid, spread, notional imbalance across the visible levels, and usable depth. The tilt is deliberately small and slow — it speaks only when the book genuinely takes a side. Honest caveat: displayed depth is an advertisement — traders can cancel in milliseconds, and a giant one-sided read may be spoofed interest that vanishes on approach. The live scoreboard above is the verdict, not the theory.
