Crowd mood — Reddit sentiment signal noisy experimental

Sasha reads the newest post titles from r/XRP and r/CryptoCurrency (public pages, no keys) and counts mood words against a pre-registered, frozen word list. The score is normalized against its own recent history, so a strong read means strong relative to usual Reddit. The tilt is deliberately tiny — this is the noisiest feed in the model. Currently at weight zero: scored, never used, until Sasha finds out-of-sample evidence. Honest caveat: the word list can't read sarcasm, threads can be brigaded, and crowd mood lags price as often as it leads it.
